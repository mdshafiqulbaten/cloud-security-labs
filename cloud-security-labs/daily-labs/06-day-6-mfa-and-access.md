# Day 6: MFA and access

*Auto-extracted from "Cloud Security: A Beginner's Guide to Protecting AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud" (source lines 8884-8903). Review against the printed book before classroom use.*

Today is about protecting the front door. Review MFA, strong
authentication, least privilege, and emergency access.

Make a list of accounts that would cause the most damage if stolen:
root, owner, global administrator, billing admin, and service accounts.

Write a short access rule: who gets admin power, when they get it,
how it is approved, and when it is removed.

Evidence to save: a safe MFA and least-privilege checklist with no
usernames or real account screenshots.

Safety check: never lock yourself out of a lab account. Keep recovery
methods updated before changing authentication settings.

End by choosing one identity that deserves the strongest protection.
